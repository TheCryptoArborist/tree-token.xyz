CORE CONSUMER SAFEGUARD

No purchase necessary

No purchase or payment will be necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not improve a participant’s chance of winning.

The public launch must provide a free entry method with the same entry limit and the same chance of selection as any purchase-linked method. That free-entry workflow is being built and must pass verification before public entries open.

TREE purchases remain ordinary token transactions with market risk. They must never be represented as a requirement for raffle participation or as a way to improve the odds of winning.

How winner selection works

  1. Entry period closes.No additional tickets can be added after the daily ledger is locked.
  2. Ledger is committed.The ordered ticket ranges are reduced to a SHA-256 commitment before randomness is requested.
  3. Sui selects one ticket.The published Move contract requests one-time Sui on-chain randomness and permanently records the winning ticket.
  4. Winner is verified.The winning wallet must own the selected range in the committed ledger before the 50,000 TREE prize is reserved.
  5. Winner claims.The reserved prize can be claimed once and only by the recorded winning wallet.

Odds depend on the total number of eligible entries recorded for that daily period. If no eligible entries exist, no winner is selected and no prize is awarded for that period.

Prize and claim conditions

  • The prize is 50,000 units of the official TREE coin on Sui Mainnet.
  • The prize is not cash and no fixed U.S. dollar value is promised.
  • A winner must control the recorded Sui wallet and complete the claim transaction.
  • A reserved prize cannot be withdrawn by the raffle administrator.
  • Any taxes or reporting obligations are the winner’s responsibility, subject to applicable law.
  • No fee will be charged by TREE to receive a prize.
  • A normal Sui network gas cost may appear when a wallet submits an on-chain claim.
  • A winner unable to complete the wallet claim must contact the project through an official channel for assistance.
  • Never send funds or disclose a seed phrase to claim a TREE prize.
  • Announcements are valid only when they match the on-chain and site records.

Eligibility and conduct

The public eligibility territory and sponsor details are not yet effective. The public promotion will be limited to adults who are legally permitted to participate in their place of residence and will be void where prohibited. The Sponsor may disqualify entries produced through bots, duplicate identities or wallets, false information, manipulation, exploits, sanctions evasion, or interference with the entry, ledger, draw, or claim systems.

Employees, contractors, administrators, and household members involved in operating or auditing the draw will not be eligible for public prizes. Blockchain outages, unavailable randomness, security incidents, or other events that prevent a fair draw may delay a round; they will never authorize a private reroll of an already executed draw.
