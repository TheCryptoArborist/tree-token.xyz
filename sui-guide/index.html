FOUR TERMS TO KNOW

Plain language before you begin

Wallet

An app that holds your assets and asks you to approve every transaction.

Sui network

The blockchain where SUI, TREE, NFTrees, and the Command Center operate.

Network fee

A small amount of SUI paid to process an action. Keep some SUI unspent.

Liquidity deposit

Supplying a pair of assets to a trading pool so other users can swap between them.

FOUR SAFE STEPS

Move SUI from Coinbase to your wallet

  1. 1

    Create or open your wallet

    Use one of the official wallet links above. Secure your recovery method privately. Never share a recovery phrase, password, private key, or verification code with anyone.

  2. 2

    Copy your Sui receive address

    Inside the wallet, choose Receive and select the Sui network. In a multi-network wallet such as Nightly, do not copy a Solana, Ethereum, or other network address. A Sui address normally begins with 0x.

  3. 3

    Send SUI from Coinbase

    In Coinbase, choose Send crypto, select SUI, paste your Sui receive address, and select the Sui network if Coinbase asks you to choose a network.

  4. 4

    Send a small test first

    Review the network and compare the first and last characters of the address. Send a small test amount, wait for it to appear in your wallet, and only then consider sending the remainder.

!
On-chain transfers are irreversible

An incorrect address or network can permanently lose funds. Network fees and regional restrictions may apply. Keep a small amount of SUI available for future Sui transaction fees.

YOUR FIRST TREE ACTION

Buy TREE inside the Command Center

Once your SUI arrives, the rest happens inside the TREE app. You can review the expected amount and route before your wallet asks for approval.

  1. 1
    Connect the funded wallet

    Use the same Slush, Nightly, or compatible Sui wallet that received your SUI.

  2. 2
    Choose how much SUI to swap

    The Swap screen shows the estimated TREE amount and dollar values before anything is submitted.

  3. 3
    Review and approve

    Confirm the route and amounts, then approve the transaction inside your wallet. Keep a little SUI for network fees.
