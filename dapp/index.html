Rules, ticket preview & verification

How It Works

  • Minimum qualifying purchase: $5 equivalent
  • Main tickets use a sub-linear 0.9457 exponent
  • One Lucky Leaf ticket per qualifying transaction
  • Potential one-time streak bonuses on days 7 and 15
  • Qualifying tickets enter both active rounds

Ticket Formula Preview

Simulation only. Results are not recorded, connected to the swap, or valid tickets.

Fairness & Methodology

Every finalized buy must be independently verified. The ticket ledger must be publicly committed before one-time Sui randomness is requested, and the winning wallet must match that committed ticket range.

finalized SUI → TREE buy → effects independently verified → tickets committed → round closes → one on-chain draw → proof-bound winner → reserved prize claimed

Launch Requirements

Prize funding, official rules, public randomness, and claims must be approved before activation.
