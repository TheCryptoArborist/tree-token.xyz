About TREE

TREE is the utility token at the center of the Thickquidity ecosystem on Sui. It connects liquidity, NFTree access, holder reward infrastructure, games, and future applications through a growing set of independently usable products.

The ecosystem currently includes NFTree minting, TreeDrop holder rewards, Garden Battles, TREE liquidity across recognized Sui markets, and future utilities including Arboretum and TREE Prediction Games.

First TREE Fund Donation Completed

Real-World Impact

NFTree growth supports arboricultural research.

On May 23, 2026, the Thickquidity and NFTree community completed its first documented contribution to TREE Fund. The official acknowledgment records a $103.00 gift plus $4.05 in processing support, for a total contribution of $107.05.

The receipt identifies the contribution as representing 5% of NFTree sales. Five percent of NFTree sale proceeds continues to be allocated to TREE Fund in support of research, scholarships, and community-engagement programs.

The receipt is preserved as issued and displays the project’s former tree-token.net domain. Tree-token.xyz is the current official TREE website.
