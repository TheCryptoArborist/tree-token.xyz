Frequently asked questions

What is TREE?

TREE is the utility token at the center of the Thickquidity ecosystem on Sui. It connects liquidity, NFTree access, holder rewards, games, identity, and planned future applications.

How do I buy TREE?

Open the TREE Command Center and use the Swap section or follow the official SuiDex route. Always verify the complete TREE coin type before approving a transaction.

What is NFTree?

NFTree is a 25 SUI NFT access asset. It can provide eligibility for TreeDrop reward rounds, access to Garden Battles, identity features, referral qualification, and future TREE utilities.

What is TreeDrop?

TreeDrop is the holder-reward distribution tool. A wallet connects, the site detects eligible NFTrees, and each eligible NFTree can claim once per applicable reward round.

How is the Canopy Board calculated?

The verified board ranks eligible wallets by total TREE exposure: liquid TREE plus current principal TREE attributed to supported SuiDex V2, SuiDex V3, and Turbos LP positions. Partial scans do not publish rankings.

How is burned TREE measured?

The site reports TREE held by the Sui zero address as burned. Circulating supply is calculated as the fixed total supply minus burned TREE.

Is the Canopy Draw raffle live?

No. The Canopy Draw remains in development. No entries are currently accepted, no prizes are offered, and final rules require legal, technical, funding, and operational approval.

Which features are live today?

TREE market and liquidity access, NFTree minting, TreeDrop reward checks, Garden Battles, the verified Canopy Board, behavioral badges, wallet management, and Profile Studio are live.

Where are the official TREE documents and links?

Use the Documents page for the litepaper, complete coin type, Noodles market page, CoinGecko listing, and official social channels.
