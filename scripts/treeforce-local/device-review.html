Listen and play

  1. Start in portrait. Read the guide, then try Fire, Auto Fire and Shield. Does each control feel clear and easy to reach?
  2. Listen to shots, impacts and music together. Try reduced volume, mute, and sound on again.
  3. Pause and resume. Switch apps or lock your phone, then return. Check for stuck firing, missing audio or unexpected movement.
  4. Rotate the phone and return to portrait. Check the hero, score, Pause button and bottom controls.
  5. Play through growing the hero and a boss encounter. Check enemy warnings, movement and room to dodge.
  6. Read the wave and challenge result cards. Is it clear which points you earned?
