Truck Engine #2 — engine-sample.js
Recording by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, sound 1443.
Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/truck-engine-2-s1443.html
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication), as stated on the source page.
License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
Source and license checked November 5, 2026 (America/Chicago).

Adaptation: extracted 4.25 seconds starting at 4 seconds, resampled to mono
22050 Hz, high-pass filtered at 38 Hz, low-pass filtered at 4300 Hz,
removed DC, crossfaded 0.25 seconds at the loop join, and normalized the
peak to 0.72. The resulting four-second PCM16 loop is encoded as base64
inside engine-sample.js for offline file:// playback. Source recording
identifies a truck engine, not a specific truck make or engine model.
