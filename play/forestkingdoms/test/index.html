A GUARDIAN'S FIELD GUIDE

Lead the way.

Move
WASD, arrow keys, or click / tap a clearing. On touch screens, drag the movement pad.
Strike
Space or Strike. Move close to an enemy; the hero faces the nearest threat.
Root snare
Q or Root snare. Holds nearby enemies in place. Ready again after six seconds.
Interact
E or Interact. Approach characters, the bridge, supply cache, grove, or workshop.
Pause
Escape or the pause button. Dialogue also pauses the action.
