# Tree Hero movement atlas prompts — first pass

Date: November 19, 2026 (America/Chicago)

Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the owner's canonical Tree Hero artwork and Willowmere Concept 01. East and west also used the generated south atlas as an identity/layout reference. Each final source atlas was requested with transparency and retained without repainting or alpha modification. The earlier three-quarter study with yellow highlights was discarded from the production set.

These are first-pass animation assets for review. They are not an assertion of completed FK-02 or physical-phone certification.

## south

Use case: stylized-concept; production transparent 2D game animation sprite atlas. Image 1 is the CANONICAL Tree Hero identity reference: keep his broad shoulders, thick athletic torso and thighs, PURPLE body/head, GREEN vine musculature and chest details, luminous GREEN eyes, GOLD armor/boots/bracers and long GOLD cape. Image 2 provides the illustrated forest game's painted outlines and slightly overhead gameplay projection. Produce only this same character, no weapon, full body in every frame, natural human limb lengths, exactly two arms and two legs. Cape attached consistently behind shoulders, never covers all stepping legs. Warm light from upper-left.
Transparent PNG with actual alpha, portrait 1024x1536 canvas. EXACTLY 12 full-body figures in a uniform FOUR COLUMNS by THREE ROWS grid. Each 256x512 cell contains one figure at the same scale, about 400px tall, head near y55 and feet near y455 within cell. Generous empty transparent gutters, no character crossing cell boundaries. All figures face the same specified direction. NO TEXT, labels, frame numbers, borders, shadows, ground, scenery, gradients, decorative backdrop, or drawn checkerboard. Only isolated opaque character pixels with clean antialiased edges and genuine transparent space.
Row1 = four subtle idle breathing frames with planted identical feet and gentle cape changes.
Row2 plus Row3 = one continuous eight-frame WALK IN PLACE cycle, read left to right: right-foot contact, weight down, left-foot passing, left-foot forward/up, LEFT-foot contact, weight down, RIGHT-foot passing, right-foot forward/up returning into frame1. Row3 MUST lead with the opposite leg from row2; the two rows are different halves of ONE full natural stride. Alternating opposite arm swing, knees bend, feet separate. Modest walk rather than running or marching, gentle vertical bob, consistent face, costume, body mass, and camera. Character center does not drift in cell. Polished illustrated fantasy action-game sprites, not photographic 3D. Readable at88px tall. DIRECTION: SOUTH, facing toward the viewer/bottom of screen. Frontal body view with a slightly elevated camera showing the top of head and shoulders; face and chest visible. Both eyes GREEN. Feet step toward and away from the viewer. Small perspective depth on far leg, consistent 25-degree overhead angle.
